Privacy Policy for Time Pass

Effective Date: October 2, 2026 | Last Updated: October 2, 2026

Welcome to Time Pass ("the Application", "we", "our", or "us"). We are dedicated to respecting and protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines what information is collected, how it is used, and how your privacy is safeguarded when you download, install, and use the Time Pass mobile application on Android.

Summary: Time Pass is a casual, sensory touch relaxation application. We do not require account registration or login. Your gameplay data, custom settings, and touch preferences remain 100% private on your own device. We utilize Google AdMob to display banner and interstitial/app open advertisements, which may collect non-personally identifiable diagnostic and advertising data in accordance with Google's policies.

1. Information We Do Not Collect (Zero Personal Tracking)

We believe in absolute data minimization. Time Pass does not collect, store, or sell any of the following personal information:

  • Your name, physical address, or phone number
  • Your personal email address or login credentials
  • Your biometric information or private photos
  • Financial details or payment credentials
  • Precise GPS location data

2. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

All data authoring and personalization features within Time Pass are stored purely locally on your device via internal Android database storage:

  • Session Metrics: Elapsed playtime, total touch/tap counts, session start/end timestamps, and custom session titles.
  • Customization Preferences: Selected color themes, touch pad shapes, ripple visual effects, sound frequencies, and vibration intensity.
  • Custom Pad Images: Any image you pick through the Android Photo Picker is stored in private internal app storage solely to display inside your touch pad. It is never uploaded or shared with any server.

You can delete or reset this data at any time from within the application or by clearing the app data in Android Settings.

3. Third-Party Services & Advertising (Google AdMob)

To keep Time Pass free to play, the Application integrates third-party software development kits (SDKs), specifically Google AdMob.

Google AdMob may collect and process certain device and usage information to serve advertisements, including:

  • Device Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (GAID), Android ID, device manufacturer, model, operating system version, and system language.
  • Ad Performance & Diagnostics: Ad impressions, click activity, ad load errors, and general technical performance.
  • Coarse Location: Approximate location derived from your network IP address to serve geographically relevant ads.

For more information on how Google collects, uses, and protects your information, please review:

  • Google Privacy & Terms
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services
  • AdMob Privacy & Security Policy

4. Permissions Used by the Application

Time Pass requests only the minimal permissions required for operation:

  • android.permission.INTERNET: Required to communicate with Google Mobile Ads servers to request and display banner and app open / interstitial ads.
  • android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Used by Google AdMob to check whether an active internet connection is available before requesting ads.
  • android.permission.VIBRATE: Used solely to generate tactile haptic vibrations when you interact with the touch pad.

5. Opting Out of Personalized Advertising

You have full control over personalized advertising on your Android device:

  • Reset Advertising ID: On Android, go to Settings → Google → Ads → Reset advertising ID.
  • Delete Advertising ID / Opt Out of Personalization: Go to Settings → Google → Ads → Delete advertising ID (or toggle "Opt out of Ads Personalization" on older Android versions). When opted out, you will still see ads, but they will not be based on your interests.

6. Children’s Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Time Pass does not knowingly collect, solicit, or maintain personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately at the email address below so we can take appropriate measures to delete any such data.

7. Security of Your Data

Because all gameplay settings and history remain strictly on your local device, your data is protected by the Android sandbox security architecture. We do not operate external web servers or databases that store your personal gameplay files.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our app features or applicable legal and regulatory requirements. Any modifications will be posted here with an updated "Last Updated" date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
